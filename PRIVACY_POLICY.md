# Mistra Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 3, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 3, 2026  

At **Mistra** ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the Mistra mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By creating an account or using Mistra, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide and improve our productivity services.

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Credentials:** When you register or sign in, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials (passwords are cryptographically hashed and never stored in plain text).
- **User Content:** All productivity items created inside the app, including tasks, subtasks, habits, notes, calendar events, due dates, reminder preferences, and category tags.
- **Profile Data:** Optional profile photos or avatars you choose to upload.
- **Support Inquiries:** Information and communication details you provide when contacting customer support.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & Usage Information:** Basic device identifiers, operating system version, app version, and crash logs to ensure app stability and performance.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Device push tokens when you opt-in to receive task reminders, habit notifications, or security alerts.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **Core Functionality:** To create and manage your account, authenticate your sessions, and securely sync your tasks, habits, notes, and calendar events across all your devices.
2. **Reminders & Alerts:** To deliver scheduled reminders and notifications that you configure within the app.
3. **App Improvement & Security:** To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent activity, software errors, or unauthorized access.
4. **Customer Support:** To troubleshoot technical issues and respond to your questions or feedback.

**We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or user content to advertisers or third parties.**

---

## 3. Data Storage & Third-Party Infrastructure

We use trusted, industry-leading infrastructure partners to operate Mistra securely:

- **Supabase (Database & Authentication):** Your account data, authentication tokens, and user content are hosted in enterprise-grade cloud databases with encryption at rest and in transit (SSL/TLS).
- **Expo / Push Notification Services:** Used exclusively to route local and push notifications to your device when enabled.

All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to keep your data confidential and secure.

---

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

- **Retention:** We retain your account information and content for as long as your account remains active.
- **Account Deletion:** You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time directly through the app (*Profile → Delete Account*) or by emailing [support@mistra.app](mailto:support@mistra.app). Upon confirmation, your data is permanently purged from our active databases.

---

## 5. Security of Your Information

We employ administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information, including:
- End-to-end encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
- Strict database-level Row Level Security (RLS), ensuring that only authenticated users can access their own workspace data.
- Secure token-based session management.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Choices (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights:
- **Access & Portability:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction:** Update or correct your profile details directly in the app.
- **Deletion:** Request permanent erasure of your personal data.
- **Opt-Out:** Disable push notifications at any time in device settings or within the app (*Profile → Notifications*).

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Mistra does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
